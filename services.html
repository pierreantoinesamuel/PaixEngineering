01

Design for Manufacturing

We review designs through the lens of real processes, materials, tooling, cost, and repeatability. The earlier this conversation starts, the more options your team keeps.

  • Part and assembly reviews
  • Material and process selection
  • Cost and risk reduction
02

FEA & Simulation

Use analysis to answer the questions that are expensive to learn on the factory floor. We make load paths, failure modes, and design tradeoffs visible.

  • Structural and static analysis
  • Design iteration support
  • Engineering documentation
03

Electronics Thermal Management

Thermal performance is a product requirement, not a late-stage rescue mission. We help teams protect reliability from enclosure to operating environment.

  • Thermal path reviews
  • Cooling and enclosure strategy
  • Validation planning
04

GD&T & Tolerance Analysis

Turn design intent into clear production language. We help define what matters, what can float, and what must be controlled.

  • Drawing reviews
  • Stack-up analysis
  • Inspection-ready requirements
05

Prototyping & Validation

Build the right thing to learn the right thing. We support prototype definition, test planning, and the iteration that turns a question into evidence.

  • Prototype strategy
  • Test and validation planning
  • Failure learning loops
06

Fixturing & Tool Development

When repeatability matters, the process needs a physical point of reference. We develop practical fixtures and tools for the work ahead.

  • Assembly fixtures
  • Manufacturing aids
  • Production handoff support
